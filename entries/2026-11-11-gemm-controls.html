Cache reuse matters

Four 45 MiB weight banks (180 MiB total) narrowed the decode gate/up advantage from roughly 61–64% to 6–7%. Cycling banks is not a formally verified cold-cache flush. Event intervals are not interchangeable with individual profiler kernel durations.

The valid inference

The selected standalone GEMMs do not show anything like a 28× slowdown. That makes a large runtime contribution plausible. But these tests do not cover all kernels or the exact dispatches selected inside vLLM, so they cannot certify equal total GPU compute time.

Evidence labels

Recorded summary families: HOLDOUT-SUMMARY, TARGET-HOLDOUT-SUMMARY, holdout-v2-process-outcomes, target-holdout-process-outcomes. Nine process exit receipts were checked. See publication and replay limitations.
