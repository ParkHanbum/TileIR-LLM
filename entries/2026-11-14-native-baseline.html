Boundaries of the claim

Early runs that consumed supplied decode tokens or stored intermediate tensors were module diagnostics, not self-generation. They are not counted here. General EOS handling, public logprobs, and broad model/input support remain unverified. The full correctness diagnostic's elapsed time includes validation and is not an inference performance number.
